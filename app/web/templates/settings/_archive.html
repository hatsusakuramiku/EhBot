{# 归档 tab: what happens to a file between「下载完成」and「CBZ 入库」. Four independent forms, each posting to the endpoint it has always posted to. They are separate rather than one 保存 button because they fail separately: a qBittorrent address that cannot be reached must not stop the operator from raising a decompression limit. The qBittorrent password field is empty on load and its placeholder says whether one is stored -- never the value. Blank means「不修改」, which is why it cannot be a required field. The safety limits are the decompression-bomb gate. This form can only ever raise or lower a number the extractor already enforces before reading an archive; it cannot switch the gate off, and there is deliberately no field here that would. #}
