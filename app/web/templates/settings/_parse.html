{# 解析规则 tab: 一条消息够不够格成为候选, and the optional AI gate over it. Two forms, both server-validated, both posted to their own route. The parse scheme is a whitelist of message kinds -- 「只解析含 Eh 链接的消息」 is the shipped default -- and the AI gate is the semantic second opinion that runs *before* it. They are on one page because they are one pipeline; an operator who turns on AI admission and cannot see the parse rules beside it will reasonably wonder why a photo-filled message was still dropped. Every checkbox is a real checkbox and the 恢复默认 button is a real submit, so the tab works with JavaScript off. Nothing here is a state label: the values come from `解析规则`'s snapshot, and the words beside them are page copy. #}
